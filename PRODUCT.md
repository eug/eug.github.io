# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers visiting a personal technical blog to understand ideas about software, AI, and engineering. They need a quiet reading experience that works well across desktop and mobile browsers.

## Product Purpose

Publish thoughtful, long-form technical writing through a small, transparent static-site generator. Success means the writing remains easy to read, share, archive, and consume without unnecessary application complexity.

## Brand Personality

Curious, restrained, and technically direct. The site should feel personal and handmade without becoming decorative or nostalgic.

## Anti-references

Avoid publication layouts crowded with promotional modules, app-like interface chrome, decorative cards, attention-seeking motion, and controls that compete with the essay.

## Design Principles

- Keep the writing primary and supporting tools quiet.
- Prefer transparent file conventions over configuration.
- Use progressive enhancement so core content remains available without JavaScript.
- Add interaction only when it gives the reader meaningful control.
- Preserve the site's lightweight, understandable implementation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible labels, keyboard-operable controls, sufficient contrast, and responsive layouts. Core reading and audio playback must remain usable without animation or pointer-specific interaction.
